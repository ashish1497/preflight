# Preflight — 60-second MVP video script

Record at 1440×900, browser zoom 100%, a fresh browser profile (empty Shipments). Keep the mouse slow.
Uses features from the execution plan (meter, chat file drop, checklist, network map). If one isn't built by the freeze, skip that shot and add 2–3 s to the neighbouring shot.
Sample file: `public/sample/ShipCheck_sample_bookings.xlsx` (also downloadable from Bulk check → "Download sample").

| Time | On screen | Do / type / say | Caption (on-screen text) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:05 | Title card: navy, "Preflight" wordmark | — | **Mistakes are made at booking. Found at the border. Days later.** |
| 0:05–0:15 | New booking → chat. Type in the box | Type: `3 pallets of ceramic mugs from Manchester to an Amazon warehouse in Frankfurt, ready 5 Nov. 1080 mugs at £5, 12 per box, half a kilo each, made in the UK. Cheapest please.` → Send | **Say it in your own words.** |
| 0:15–0:22 | Reply with numbered questions; booking sheet filling on the right | Pick the HS code option `69120023` (stoneware), click **About 660 kg** (Recommended), **Yes, it's booked** → Send answers | **It asks only what's missing — with a recommendation.** |
| 0:22–0:27 | Pre-flight meter + carrier card | Type: `Actually make it 4 pallets` → Send. Hold on the meter, pallets field flash, carrier price updating | **Change anything. Every check re-runs.** |
| 0:27–0:33 | Drag `ShipCheck_sample_bookings.xlsx` onto the chat | Drop the file; summary card appears (ready / review / blocked counts, top issues) | **Or drop your booking sheet.** |
| 0:33–0:39 | Click **Review in Bulk check** → BK-102 | Click **Apply: Quantity → 950**, then **Apply: Declared value**, then **Apply: Total cartons → 62** — status turns Ready | **What's wrong, how serious, how to fix — in one click.** |
| 0:39–0:43 | Open BK-107, carrier card | Hover the ruled-out Carrier C note | **Fastest is express — but 480 kg pallets exceed its 400 kg limit. So: Carrier B, and why.** |
| 0:43–0:48 | Open BK-101 → Customs & documents checklist | Show the **79% anti-dumping** warning on the Chinese stoneware mug and the exemption task (licence or confirm exemption) | **Customs, decoded — per product, from the official UK Trade Tariff.** |
| 0:48–0:52 | Confirm a ready booking (BK-107) | Click **Confirm booking** → confirmation with reference, dates, next steps → **Go to Shipments** | **Booked — with what happens next.** |
| 0:52–0:57 | Shipments dashboard, network map section | Slow pan over the map; action list visible | **Every shipment, and what still needs you.** |
| 0:57–1:00 | End card | — | **79/79 planted mistakes caught · 0 false alarms. Preflight — catch it before customs does.** |

**Narration (optional voice-over, ~140 words, read at a calm pace):**
"Booking freight to the EU means long forms — and small mistakes that stop stock at the border days later. Preflight moves that check to the moment you book. Describe your shipment in plain words. It asks only what's missing, with a recommendation. Change anything, and every check re-runs. Or drop your booking sheet: each problem shows what's wrong, how serious it is, and a one-click fix. It picks a carrier for your priority and explains why. Per product, it shows official duty and documents — like a 79% anti-dumping duty on Chinese ceramics. Confirm, and you get the documents and next steps. On our seeded benchmark it caught 79 of 79 planted mistakes with no false alarms. Preflight — catch it before customs does."

**Before recording:** clear Shipments (Settings → Sync shows a fresh workspace, or use a private window); check the AI replies in under ~5 s (else record the chat part again); turn off browser notifications.
